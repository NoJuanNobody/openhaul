## 4. Viral Growth Loops & Community Incentives

1. **"Build Your Rig" Van Showcase Contests:**
   - Community bounty program encouraging drivers to showcase their mobile van builds, auxiliary battery setups, and cold-chain storage integrations on YouTube/TikTok with hashtag `#BuiltForOpenHaul`.
   - Top-rated builds receive $1,000 tool grants funded from the Consortium ecosystem pool.
2. **Proof-of-Haul Social Badges:**
   - Drivers receive verifiable on-chain/cryptographic badges for disaster missions completed, emergency miles driven, and zero-excursion cold-chain deliveries. Badges unlock priority access to high-yield relief-dispatch work (planned; no agency relationship exists).
3. **Open-Source Protocol Hackathons:**
   - Rust and mobile developers compete to optimize offline mesh routing algorithms (`stmp-core`) and explore ZK proof verification (in development) on low-power ARM devices.
